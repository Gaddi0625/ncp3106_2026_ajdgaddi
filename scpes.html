About SCPES

The Society of Computer Engineering Students (SCPES) is the recognized student organization of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering program at the University of the East — Manila.

SCPES aims to unify Computer Engineering students through quality programs and events related to Computer Engineering, emphasizing the success of a diverse student, faculty, and alumni community.

Recognized by the University of the East Manila Student Affairs Office under the College of Engineering.
