About Computer Engineering

Computer Engineering combines principles from electrical engineering and computer science to develop computing systems that integrate hardware and software.

Computer Engineering commonly emphasizes hardware-software integration, while Computer Science commonly emphasizes software, algorithms, data, and computing theory. The subjects overlap, and the balance varies by program.

Yes. Computer Engineering connects physical computing components with the software that controls and uses them.

Studying Computer Engineering

General areas include embedded systems, computer architecture, networks, cybersecurity, robotics, software development, and hardware design. Available subjects vary by institution and curriculum.

Logical thinking, problem solving, communication, mathematics, and a willingness to learn both programming and electronics are useful foundations.

Careers

Graduates may explore roles involving embedded systems, hardware, software, networks, cybersecurity, systems integration, data, cloud computing, robotics, technical support, and research.
