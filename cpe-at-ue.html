Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BSCpE) program at the University of the East educates students to understand both sides of the hardware-software interface, from designing circuits to creating operating systems. Multidisciplinary in scope, the CpE curriculum integrates the fields of electrical engineering and computer science.

The CpE curriculum and student projects focus on embedded systems — devices that use a microprocessor or microcontroller for a specific purpose. Embedded systems appear in a wide array of household and industrial systems, including portable and console game systems, robots, game peripherals, electronic toys, digital cameras, audio/video component systems, and even aircraft flight systems.

The computer engineering program at UE is a balance of engineering science and design. Freshman and sophomore year courses lay a solid foundation for junior and senior level design courses through courses in hardware, software, and engineering sciences (mathematics, physics, and chemistry).

Program Educational Objectives

The University will produce Computer Engineering graduates who are:

  1. Professionally competent and globally competitive in the field of Computer Engineering.
  2. Equipped with technical competencies and able to contribute to the country's social and economic progress.
  3. Lifelong learners imbued with the UE core values of Excellence, Integrity, Professionalism, Teamwork, Commitment, Transparency, Accountability and Social Responsibility.

Student Outcomes

Graduates of the Computer Engineering program will be able to:

  1. Apply knowledge of mathematics and science to solve complex Computer Engineering problems.
  2. Design and conduct experiments, as well as analyze and interpret data.
  3. Design a system, component, or process to meet desired needs within realistic constraints.
  4. Function on multidisciplinary teams.
  5. Identify, formulate, and solve complex Computer Engineering problems.
  6. Understand professional and ethical responsibility.
  7. Communicate effectively.
  8. Understand the impact of Computer Engineering solutions in a global, economic, environmental, and societal context.
  9. Recognize the need for, and engage in, life-long learning.
  10. Know contemporary issues.
  11. Use techniques, skills, and modern engineering tools necessary for Computer Engineering practice.
  12. Understand Computer Engineering and management principles as a member and leader in a team.

Why Study CpE at UE?

The University of the East's Computer Engineering program offers distinct advantages rooted in its history, facilities, and industry connections.

Accredited Quality

Caloocan Campus: PAASCU Level III Re-Accredited. Manila Campus: PACUCOA Level II Candidate Status.

Embedded Systems Focus

Curriculum centered on embedded systems — the computing technology in everyday devices — with hands-on projects from freshman year.

Licensure Performance

UE Manila reported a first-timers passing rate of 93.33% in the 2025 Electrical Engineers Licensure Exam (which includes CpE).

Industry-Recognized Graduates

Graduates pursue roles in embedded systems, networks, software, and emerging fields across local and international industries.

UE Core Values

Excellence, Integrity, Professionalism, Teamwork, Commitment, Transparency, Accountability, and Social Responsibility integrated throughout the program.

Manila & Caloocan Campuses

Two campuses providing access to Metro Manila's tech ecosystem and industry partnerships.
